- **A letter layout** (`cover_layout: "letter"` with the document's `letter`, [spec 0002 §4.2](docs/specs/0002-template-slots.md)). No cover page, no control page and, unless asked for, no contents list: page 1 opens with a letterhead (the sender's logo on the left; the name, address and date on the right), an accent rule, the title and the addressee; the body follows, then a signature block (a 58 mm by 16 mm space with a rule along its foot, an optional signature image, the name and title) and a numbered list of enclosures, each kept on one page; appendices follow on their own pages. The same in PDF, HTML and Word, from one table of measurements in the core (`render::LETTER`). In Word the letter is one section, the letterhead a borderless two-column table, and every paragraph is in a `Letter*` style written only when used, so the file can be the editable master of the letter. `ORD024` refuses the layout with no `letter`, a `letter` with another layout, and a letter that names a cover photograph, logos or cover rows; `ORD001` and `ORD011` cover the new field and its images. A new example, `examples/letter.json`, joins the conformance suite.
