- **A document head instead of a cover page** (`cover_layout: "head"`, [spec 0002 §4.1](docs/specs/0002-template-slots.md)). The organisation as a kicker, the title, the subtitle and notice, an accent rule and the summary open page 1, and the body follows on the same page: the shape of a short briefing or memo. The same in PDF, HTML and Word (Word writes `Head*` styles only when used). A head that names a cover photograph, logos or cover rows is refused (`ORD023`). Also two template values, `data_ground` and `data_rule`, for the ground and rule colour of a data box. A new example, `examples/briefing-note.json`, joins the conformance suite.
- **A data box with no items prints its notes as its text.** The PDF writer printed a box's notes small and grey, and Word in table text, even when the box had no items for them to annotate, so a box of prose read as a footnote. With no items, the notes are now body text in the ink in every writer. A box with items is unchanged.
