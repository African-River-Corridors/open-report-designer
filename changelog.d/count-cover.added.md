- **Page numbers that skip the cover** (format 0.2). A document's `count_cover: false` leaves the cover out of the page numbers: the page after the cover is page 1, and the total counts only the pages after it, so a cover and eight pages read "Page 1 of 8" to "Page 8 of 8". The contents list uses the same numbers. In Word the body section starts at 1 and the total is its page count (`SECTIONPAGES`). The default, `true`, is unchanged, and a document with no cover is unchanged either way.
